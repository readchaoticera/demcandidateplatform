Position

Reading this well

  • Position combines three kinds of evidence by precedence. Cosponsoring the bill — a recorded legislative act — outranks a candidate’s own campaign site, which outranks news coverage. Every row records which one decided it, and the campaign-site-only reading is shown inside the row for comparison.
  • That gap is the finding. Most cosponsors never mention the bill to voters, so the campaign-site number is less than half the combined one.
  • Districts may carry several candidates. Louisiana runs an all-party ballot with no nominating primary, and California, Washington and Alaska can send more than one Democrat to the same general election. Those are not duplicates.
  • News-sourced rows are the weakest evidence and are labelled in the row detail, including where a source was thin or contradictory.
