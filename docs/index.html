Reading this well. “Position” combines three kinds of evidence by precedence: cosponsoring the bill (a recorded legislative act) outranks a candidate’s own campaign site, which outranks news coverage. Every row records which one decided it. The campaign-site-only reading is shown inside each row for comparison — it is a much lower number, because most cosponsors never mention the bill to voters.

Louisiana runs an all-party ballot with no nominating primary, and California, Washington and Alaska can send more than one Democrat to the same general election — so districts may carry several candidates here, and that is correct rather than a duplicate.
